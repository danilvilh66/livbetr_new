What is included in the monthly price?

Your medication, clinician review and ongoing oversight, a 1:1 care coach, check-ins, progress tracking and free delivery. There are no extra fees for dose increases.

Does my price go up when my dose goes up?

No. Semaglutide is $65 a month and tirzepatide is $110 a month at every dose, for as long as you stay on your plan.

When am I charged?

Payment is collected at checkout, before your clinician review. Paying does not guarantee approval. If your clinician does not approve treatment, your full initial payment is refunded within seven business days.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel by email at support@livbetr.com, in the patient portal or by phone at 1-888-664-9435. Cancellation takes effect when we receive it, and nobody will try to talk you out of it.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are generally available until the pharmacy starts preparing your medication. Approved refunds are submitted within seven business days. See the full refund and cancellation policy.
