Getting started

Do I qualify?

You may qualify if your BMI is 30 or higher, or 27 or higher with a weight-related condition such as high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes or high cholesterol. You must be 18 or older. A licensed clinician makes the final decision after reviewing your intake. Check your eligibility in about 3 minutes.

How long does it take to get started?

The eligibility check takes about 3 minutes and the full intake about 15. Same-day review slots are available, and a clinician reviews your answers before anything is prescribed.

Which states do you serve?

We are expanding quickly. Your state is checked during sign-up, before you pay.

Is Livbetr legitimate?

Yes. Livbetr is LegitScript certified and works only with licensed U.S. clinicians and accredited U.S. pharmacies. Livbetr is a technology and administrative platform: it does not practice medicine or dispense medication itself. Clinicians in our partner network make every prescribing decision.

Do I need insurance?

No. Your monthly price covers your medication and clinician review, with or without insurance.

Medication and safety

What is the difference between semaglutide and tirzepatide?

Semaglutide acts on one hormone pathway (GLP-1) that helps regulate appetite and blood sugar. Tirzepatide acts on two (GLP-1 and GIP), so it works on appetite in two ways. Your clinician will recommend the one that fits your health history and goals.

Are compounded medications FDA-approved?

No. Compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are prepared by licensed pharmacies for individual patients and are not FDA-approved. The FDA does not review compounded drugs for safety, effectiveness or quality. Read our GLP-1 safety information before you start.

How does dosing work?

Most people start on a low dose that increases gradually as the body adjusts. Your clinician sets and adjusts your dose based on your progress and how you feel. Your monthly price stays the same at every dose.

What side effects should I expect?

The most common side effects are nausea, diarrhea, constipation and reduced appetite, which often ease as your body adjusts. Serious side effects are rare but possible. Message your care team in the patient portal about any symptoms. In an emergency, call 911. For possible poisoning, call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222.

How fast will I see results?

Most members see results within the first few weeks. Weight loss is gradual and individual results vary. If progress stalls, your clinician reviews your plan and can adjust your treatment.

Pricing and billing

What is included in the monthly price?

Your medication, clinician review and ongoing oversight, a 1:1 care coach, check-ins, progress tracking and free delivery. There are no extra fees for dose increases.

Does my price go up when my dose goes up?

No. Semaglutide is $65 a month and tirzepatide is $110 a month at every dose, for as long as you stay on your plan.

When am I charged?

Payment is collected at checkout, before your clinician review. Paying does not guarantee approval. If your clinician does not approve treatment, your full initial payment is refunded within seven business days.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel by email at support@livbetr.com, in the patient portal or by phone at 1-888-664-9435. Cancellation takes effect when we receive it, and nobody will try to talk you out of it.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are generally available until the pharmacy starts preparing your medication. Approved refunds are submitted within seven business days. See the full refund and cancellation policy.

Delivery and refills

How is my medication shipped?

Free and discreet, in plain packaging, directly to your door. A new supply ships every 4 weeks.

What if my delivery is late, missing or damaged?

Tell us within seven calendar days of the expected delivery date and we will make it right. Call 1-888-664-9435 or email support@livbetr.com. Prescription medication cannot be returned for resale.

How do refills work?

Refills ship automatically on schedule and are timed to arrive before your current supply runs out. You can change or cancel your plan at any time.

Account and support

Sign in to the patient portal with the email you used at sign-up. If you are locked out, call us and we will walk you through it.

How do I contact Livbetr?

For billing, orders and account help, call 1-888-664-9435 (Monday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. ET) or email support@livbetr.com. For questions about your medication, message your clinical care team in the patient portal.

A licensed clinician reviews your intake. If treatment is approved, the pharmacy prepares your medication and ships it to you. If it is not approved, your payment is refunded in full.
