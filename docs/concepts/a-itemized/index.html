The fine print, printed large

Compounded medications are not FDA-approved. They’re prepared by licensed U.S. pharmacies; the FDA doesn’t review them for safety, effectiveness or quality.

You pay before a clinician reviews you. If treatment isn’t approved, your full payment is refunded within seven business days.

Results vary. Plans aim for about 15% of body weight over time. That’s a goal, not a promise.

Livbetr doesn’t practice medicine. Independent licensed clinicians make every prescribing decision. In an emergency, call 911.
