Do I qualify?

You may qualify if your BMI is 30 or higher, or 27 or higher with a weight-related condition such as high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes or high cholesterol. You must be 18 or older. A licensed clinician makes the final decision after reviewing your intake. Check your eligibility in about 3 minutes.

Which states do you serve?

We are expanding quickly. Your state is checked during sign-up, before you pay.

Is Livbetr legitimate?

Yes. Livbetr is LegitScript certified and works only with licensed U.S. clinicians and accredited U.S. pharmacies. Livbetr is a technology and administrative platform: it does not practice medicine or dispense medication itself. Clinicians in our partner network make every prescribing decision.

What is the difference between semaglutide and tirzepatide?

Semaglutide acts on one hormone pathway (GLP-1) that helps regulate appetite and blood sugar. Tirzepatide acts on two (GLP-1 and GIP), so it works on appetite in two ways. Your clinician will recommend the one that fits your health history and goals.

How does dosing work?

Most people start on a low dose that increases gradually as the body adjusts. Your clinician sets and adjusts your dose based on your progress and how you feel. Your monthly price stays the same at every dose.

How fast will I see results?

Most members see results within the first few weeks. Weight loss is gradual and individual results vary. If progress stalls, your clinician reviews your plan and can adjust your treatment.

What is included in the monthly price?

Your medication, clinician review and ongoing oversight, a 1:1 care coach, check-ins, progress tracking and free delivery. There are no extra fees for dose increases.
